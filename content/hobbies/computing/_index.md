Computer science is a discipline that I find very satisfying.
There's something very immediately fulfilling about developing and troubleshooting a program, or more generally being comfortable poking around a terminal and getting the 'puter to do what you want.
I've also found that once you start customising or developing your own toolkit, it's increasingly easy to expand on it and hard to renounce that freedom.

Here you'll find more standalone programming or computer related projects, as opposed to the more documentation-esque [Handbook](../../handbook).
