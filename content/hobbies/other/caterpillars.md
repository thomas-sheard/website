There was a long period of confusion, a brief period of capture, and a period of uncertain length entailing identification.

I scooped them both up and placed them in a glass dish for a better look.
There was a brief scare that they might be drain fly larvae, which are apparently nasty little creatures that feed on decaying organic matter in drains.
My critters, however, were already roughly an inch long which (thankfully) ruled this out.
